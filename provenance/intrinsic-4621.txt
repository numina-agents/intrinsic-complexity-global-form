MAIN CLAIM
The underlying complex fusion category

       D = C(sl_3,12)^0_A,       A = 1 directsum J directsum J^2,

where J is the level-12 simple current of highest weight 12 Lambda_1,
has NO conjugate-linear strong monoidal autoequivalence fixing the
isomorphism class of every simple object. In particular D has no split
real fusion form, and no gauge in which all associator F-matrices are real,
even allowing nonunitary changes of fusion bases.

This is the bosonic full-center one-form gauging of standard unitary
SU(3)_12 Chern--Simons theory, a connected PSU(3) global-form theory with
the condensation choice specified by A. No classification of all possible
discrete theta conventions is needed or asserted. It supplies a negative
answer to programme 4390's universal claim about real F after isotropic
simple-current condensation. It is NOT an exclusion of Chern--Simons
realizations of B 11, and is NOT a Witt-invariant obstruction.

The proof uses a general permutation obstruction and two EXISTING
classification theorems. It does not classify tensor autoequivalences.

1. DEFINITIONS AND THE GENERAL PERMUTATION OBSTRUCTION

All categories are finite semisimple rigid tensor categories over C with
simple unit. A conjugate-linear tensor autoequivalence K acts on scalars
by complex conjugation and has tensorator
K_2(X,Y): K(X) tensor K(Y) -> K(X tensor Y).
Its permutation of simple isomorphism classes is denoted pi(K).
We do NOT require K^2 to be coherently the identity for the obstruction.
This strengthens what is needed to exclude split real descent.

For a braiding c, its reverse is c^rev_(X,Y)=(c_(Y,X))^(-1).
An equivalence is reverse-braided when, with tensorators inserted, it
transports c to c^rev. Let PBr(B,c) be the image of the complex-LINEAR
braided tensor autoequivalence group in Sym(Irr(B)). This records the
actual action on simple labels, not just the abstract group.

Proposition (permutation obstruction).
Let (B,c,p) be a complex braided fusion category with spherical pivotal
structure p. Assume:
 (i) p is the unique pivotal structure, after canonical identification
     of duality choices;
 (ii) the only braidings on the FIXED underlying tensor category are c
      and c^rev;
 (iii) some simple Z has nonreal ribbon twist theta_Z for (c,p);
 (iv) there exists a conjugate-linear reverse-braided tensor
      autoequivalence T with pi(T) not in PBr(B,c).
Then B has no simple-fixing conjugate-linear tensor autoequivalence,
and hence no split real fusion form or all-real F-symbol gauge.

Proof. Suppose K is such a simple-fixing conjugate-linear tensor
autoequivalence. Transporting c through K gives a braiding c^K on B,
characterized on its essentially surjective image by

 c^K_(KX,KY) = K_2(Y,X)^(-1) K(c_(X,Y)) K_2(X,Y).

Naturality, the two hexagons and their inverses transport because K is
a strong tensor equivalence. Thus c^K is a braiding on the same tensor
category, not merely a braided structure on a different presentation.
Transporting dualities and p similarly gives a pivotal structure p^K.
By (i), it is p. The ribbon twist is a functorial expression in c, p,
evaluation and coevaluation, so

 theta^(c^K,p)_(KX) = K(theta^(c,p)_X).

On a simple X this scalar is conjugate(theta_X). If c^K=c, simple-fixing
of K applied to Z would give theta_Z=conjugate(theta_Z), contradicting
(iii). By (ii), c^K=c^rev. Therefore K is reverse-braided. The composition
K T is complex-linear and braided: scalar conjugation occurs twice and
braiding reversal occurs twice. Tensorators are those of the composition;
the braiding equations compose, so this is a tensor statement, not just
a statement about eigenvalues. Its simple permutation is

                  pi(K T)=pi(K) pi(T)=pi(T),

contradicting (iv). Finally a split real fusion form complexifies to B
with a simple-fixing conjugate-linear coherent involution. An all-real
F-symbol gauge defines exactly such a split real skeleton by taking the
real spans of the chosen fusion bases. Both would provide the impossible K.
This proves all conclusions. No assumption on positivity, unitarity or
square coherence of hypothetical K was used. QED.

Useful source-based sufficient hypotheses for (i)--(ii).
If B has a nondegenerate braiding, has no nontrivial invertible objects,
and is prime as a nondegenerate braided category, Nikshych [N, Cor 4.8]
gives precisely the two braidings c and c^rev on its underlying fusion
category. A spherical structure exists in our unitary application.
Trivial universal grading gives uniqueness of the pivotal structure:
the ratio of two pivotal structures is a monoidal natural automorphism
of Id. On simples its scalars obey a_X a_Y=a_Z whenever Z is a summand
of X tensor Y, so it factors through the universal grading group and is
trivial if that group is trivial. This also proves uniqueness directly,
without an unitarization assumption on an arbitrary K.

2. THE LEVEL-12 LOCAL CATEGORY: ALL CLASSIFICATION HYPOTHESES

We use the standard positive-level unitary category C=C(sl_3,12), not a
different Galois conjugate. Its integrable highest weights are
lambda=a Lambda_1+b Lambda_2, with a,b>=0 and a+b<=12. Lambda_1,Lambda_2
are the fundamental weights, with long roots of squared length 2.
The affine Dynkin triple is (12-a-b,a,b). Tensoring by J rotates this
triple. The simple current J has order 3 and twist
exp(2 pi i 12/3)=1, and its powers generate a Tannakian subcategory.
The algebra A is its regular connected commutative separable algebra.
The local-module category D=C_A^0 is unitary and nondegenerate.

Edie--Michell [E, Lemmas 2.12--2.13] describes its simples as follows.
Take J-orbits of weights with a+2 b=0 modulo 3; for each such orbit choose
a character of its J-stabilizer. A free orbit contributes one simple.
The unique fixed weight is U=4 Lambda_1+4 Lambda_2, with affine triple
(4,4,4). It contributes three distinct simples, denoted U_0,U_1,U_2.

There are 31 neutral parent weights. For an explicit finite verification,
as b runs from 0 to 12 the numbers of allowed a with a=b modulo 3 and
0<=a<=12-b are

               5,4,3,4,3,2,3,2,1,2,1,0,1,

whose sum is 31. Removing U leaves 30 weights, or 10 free orbits. Thus

                         rank(D)=10+3=13.

Every nontrivial factor in a Deligne product decomposition has rank at
least 2, and ranks multiply. Since 13 is prime, D cannot be a product of
two nontrivial nondegenerate braided factors. Thus it is prime in the
sense required by [N]. This argument needs no classification of fusion
subcategories of the parent.

In [E] notation r=2, k=12, m=3, m'=gcd(m,k)=3 and m''=m/m'=1.
The Tannakian divisibility condition m^2 | k(r+1) is 9 |36. The case is
not among [E, Thm 1.4]'s listed exceptions; in particular level 9, not 12,
is the exceptional sl_3 full-center case. Lemma 2.18 and Cor 2.19 give

           Inv(D)=Z_((r+1)/(m m''))=Z_1,
           universal grading group U(D)=Z_1.

Hence (i) and (ii) of the proposition hold, using [N, Cor 4.8]. This
invokes a theorem classifying ALL braidings on the fixed fusion
category, not just quantum-group braidings or unitary braidings.

Let Omega=F_A(Lambda_1+Lambda_2), where F_A(X)=A tensor X.
Its parent J-stabilizer is trivial, so Omega is a simple local module.
Twists descend unchanged to local modules [E, text before Lemma 2.13].
The adjoint highest weight Lambda_1+Lambda_2 has quadratic Casimir 3;
its conformal weight at level 12 is 3/(12+3)=1/5. Consequently

                       theta_Omega=exp(2 pi i/5),

which is nonreal. We use the categorical twist, not the anomaly-shifted
T-matrix entry. This verifies (iii).

3. A REVERSE-BRAIDED SEMILINEAR SYMMETRY AND ITS PERMUTATION

Galindo [G, Thm 3.7 and Lemma 3.23] supplies a coherent simple-fixing
conjugate-linear tensor involution Phi of the parent C, reversing its
braiding. The key reverse-braiding assertion is specifically Lemma 3.23,
not merely the existence of a split fusion form in Thm 3.7.

Here is the full descent argument needed to obtain T on D. On the
Tannakian subgroup H=<J>, reversal equals braiding because the braiding
is symmetric. Its split real descent has one real line in each H-degree.
In real fusion bases its associator and braiding form an abelian
3-cocycle with coefficients R^times and diagonal quadratic trace 1.
The Eilenberg--Mac Lane trace isomorphism with arbitrary abelian
coefficients, as stated in [Q, Thm 2.4], makes it an abelian coboundary
OVER R^times. Thus the ordinary graded real regular algebra R[H]
transports to a real connected commutative separable algebra A_R.
Its complexification is A. We obtain a coherent algebra identification
u:Phi(A)->A, satisfying u Phi(u)=mu_A for the square comparison
mu:Phi^2=>Id. No complex-only trivialization or square-root choice is
being substituted for real algebra descent.

For a local left module (M,r), transport its action to Phi(M) by

       r' = Phi(r) Phi_2(A,M) (u^(-1) tensor Id).

Reverse braiding transports the A/M monodromy to its inverse. Locality
is r monodromy=r; this is equivalent to r monodromy^(-1)=r. Thus the
transported action is local. On local modules the right action used for
relative tensor products can equally be written

                   r c_(M,A) = r c_(A,M)^(-1).

Therefore braiding reversal preserves the balancing relation, and
Phi_2 descends to an isomorphism
T(M) tensor_A T(N) -> T(M tensor_A N). Its coherence and the square
comparison descend by the coequalizer universal property and the
identity for u. This defines a conjugate-linear tensor autoequivalence T
of D, indeed a coherent involution, though not a split one.

T also reverses the LOCAL braiding. If q_(M,N):M tensor N ->M tensor_A N
is the quotient and c^A is the local braiding, its defining equality is

                c^A_(M,N) q_(M,N)=q_(N,M) c_(M,N).

Apply Phi, its tensorator and u to this equality. The balancing argument
just given identifies the transported quotients with the local tensor
quotients. Parent braiding reversal identifies the right side with the
formula for (c^A_(T(N),T(M)))^(-1) after quotient. Epimorphy of q then
gives exactly the reverse-braided identity for T. This adds the local
braiding statement explicitly to the checked tensor-transport account.

We next determine the permutation of T, without requiring a gauge for
its complete associator. For every parent simple X, induction intertwines
Phi and T by the algebra identification u, so

                         T(F_A(X)) isomorphic F_A(Phi(X)).

Since Phi fixes all parent simple classes, a free J-orbit contributes
a simple F_A(X) fixed by T. Thus T fixes all 10 free-orbit labels.

For the fixed weight U, F_A(U)=U_0 directsum U_1 directsum U_2.
The parent real frame on U and algebra real frame give a real frame on
F_A(U). Its endomorphism algebra is the stabilizer twisted group algebra
of [F, Prop 4.7]. More explicitly it is C_ beta[H], with a one-dimensional
complex degree for each Hom_C(U,h tensor U), h in H. These homogeneous
elements are invertible. The real involution preserves each degree:
Phi fixes every h and U, and u is block diagonal on the mutually
nonisomorphic simple summands h of A. The fixed real subalgebra E_R
therefore has one real line in each H-degree. If x is a nonzero real
element of degree J, x^3=a for a nonzero REAL scalar a. Divide x by
the real cube root of a. This gives

                      E_R = R[C_3] = R directsum C.

Its complexification is C^3, whose three primitive idempotents consist
of one real idempotent and a conjugate pair. The induced permutation of
the three distinct summands U_j therefore fixes one and transposes two.
In particular, independently of the origin chosen for the character
indices, pi(T) is an ISOLATED transposition on {U_0,U_1,U_2} and fixes
all 10 other simple labels. This calculation is also the special case
of the whole transport/odd-cyclic account independently checked in 4592.
That earlier check alone does NOT certify the intrinsic argument here.

4. THE ACTUAL LABELLED BRAIDED GROUP EXCLUDES THIS PERMUTATION

Edie--Michell [E, Thm 1.4] gives EqBr(D)=D_3, the dihedral group of
order 6. To specialize its formula, p=0 since (r+1)/(m m'')=1 has no
odd prime divisors, and t=0 since that same integer is odd. It is
important to use the labelled actions in [E, Lemmas 3.8--3.9], not only
the abstract group isomorphism. With appropriate stabilizer characters
chi_j, j in Z/3, its generators are

       R: (X,chi_j) -> (X,chi_(j+1)),
       C: (X,chi_j) -> (X^*,chi_(-j)).

For a free orbit there is just one character and R fixes the label;
C applies parent charge conjugation to the orbit. On the three fixed
resolutions R is a 3-cycle and C is a reflection. Allowing all powers
of R includes any translation of the character origin. The upper bound
from Thm 1.4 ensures there are no further braided classes with an
independently chosen permutation; these six explicitly realized classes
are the entire group, not merely a subgroup.

The free orbit of V=3 Lambda_1 is NOT fixed by charge conjugation.
Its affine coordinates are(9,3,0), while those of V^*=3 Lambda_2 are
(9,0,3). Their cyclic orbits are respectively

       {(9,3,0),(0,9,3),(3,0,9)},
       {(9,0,3),(3,9,0),(0,3,9)},

which are disjoint. Both have neutral triality and trivial stabilizer,
so give distinct simple local modules. Hence every reflection R^a C
swaps these two labels. No reflection fixes all 10 free-orbit labels.
The other three braided classes are the rotations R^a. Their action on
{U_0,U_1,U_2} is either identity or a 3-cycle, never a transposition.
Consequently the isolated transposition pi(T) is NOT in PBr(D,c^A).
This verifies hypothesis(iv) of the proposition.

For source accuracy: the text of [E, Lemma 3.8]'s proof has a displayed
line after the beta-generator calculation that repeats the reflection
formula. Its immediately preceding idempotent calculation gives the
rotation chi_j ->chi_(j+1), and the lemma statement and Lemma 3.9 state
that rotation explicitly. We use those consistent statements and the
explicit idempotent calculation, not the repeated line.

5. CONCLUSION AND EXACT STRENGTH

All four hypotheses of the general proposition are now verified for D.
If an all-real associator gauge existed, it would give a simple-fixing
conjugate-linear monoidal K. Uniqueness of the pivotal structure and
the nonreal adjoint twist force K to reverse the braiding by the
two-braiding classification. Then K T is an ordinary braided tensor
autoequivalence with the isolated transposition pi(T), contradicting
the exact labelled dihedral classification. This excludes EVERY such K,
not only those induced from parent data, and even those lacking coherent
square identity. Therefore the main claim follows.

The obstruction does NOT say D has no real form: T gives a nonsplit
real form whose fixed-point simples do not all descend separately.
It says D has no SPLIT real form, equivalently no real F-symbol gauge.
It does not assert the existence of a braided real form. The category
is itself a connected-global-form Chern--Simons example, so inherent
complexity of F cannot by itself exclude such a realization.
