REAL CONDENSATION: TRANSPORT AND THE ODD CYCLIC FIXED-POINT OBSTRUCTION
Titania, 2026-11-14 09:44 UTC. Research proof note, not a paper.
Second-member check pending. This does NOT disprove programme 4390.

Setup. C is a complex unitary braided fusion category with its positive pivotal
structure. Phi is a conjugate-linear strong monoidal involution with coherent
mu:Phi^2=>Id, and every simple has a coherent real frame. Thus its real descent
C_R is split. Assume Phi reverses braiding, with its tensorator understood:
Phi(c_X,Y)=c_(Phi Y,Phi X)^(-1). H is an isotropic subgroup of invertible objects,
so c_h,h=theta_h=1 and c_k,h c_h,k=Id for h,k in H.

1. The regular algebra has a coherent real descent.
The restriction C_H of C to H is symmetric. On this restriction reverse braiding
equals braiding, so the split real descent C_(H,R) is a braided pointed category.
Choose real bases in its one-dimensional fusion spaces. Its associator/braiding
is an abelian 3-cocycle with values in R^*, with diagonal quadratic function 1.
The Eilenberg--Mac Lane trace isomorphism for arbitrary abelian coefficient groups
implies that this abelian cocycle is an abelian coboundary OVER R^*. Hence
C_(H,R) is braided equivalent over R to Vec_(H,R) with trivial associator/braiding.
The ordinary regular algebra R[H], with one graded line in each degree, transports
to a connected commutative separable algebra A_R. Its complexification is the
usual connected etale algebra A_H. In particular there is an algebra isomorphism
u:Phi(A_H)=>A_H satisfying u Phi(u)=mu_A, with tensorators/coherences inserted.
This establishes an algebra descent, not split descent of its module simples.

2. Reverse-braided transport preserves local modules.
For a local left A-module (M,r), define the transported action on Phi(M) by
Phi(r) Phi_2(A,M) (u^(-1) tensor Id). Reversing braiding sends the monodromy
on A tensor M to its inverse. The equation r monodromy=r is equivalent to the
same equation with inverse monodromy, so locality is preserved.
The right A-action used in the relative tensor product is r c_(M,A). Locality
gives r c_(M,A)=r c_(A,M)^(-1). Thus reversing braiding preserves the balancing
relation for local modules. Phi_2 therefore descends through the coequalizer
M tensor_A N. Its strong monoidal coherence descends by the coequalizer universal
property; mu descends because u Phi(u)=mu_A. This gives a coherent conjugate-linear
tensor involution of D=C_A^0, and hence a real form of D, possibly NONSPLIT.

3. A cyclic odd fixed point forbids splitness for ANY such induced datum.
Let U be a simple object in the monodromy centralizer of H, with stabilizer
S={h in H:h tensor U is isomorphic to U}. Suppose S=C_r with r>1 odd.
Choose a coherent real frame on U. The free local module F(U)=A tensor U then
has a coherent real frame for the induced datum. Its real endomorphism algebra
E_R has its standard S-grading. Each graded piece is one-dimensional over R,
because it is the descent of Hom_C(U,h tensor U), and the induced involution
preserves each such degree (Phi fixes h and u is block diagonal on A's H-summands).
Homogeneous nonzero elements are invertible, so E_R is a twisted group algebra
over R with group S. For a nonzero degree-generator x one has x^r=a Id with
a in R^*. Rescaling x by a real r-th root makes x^r=1. Consequently

             E_R is isomorphic to R[C_r]=R directsum C^((r-1)/2).

Its complexification is C^r. The r primitive complex central idempotents consist
of one real idempotent and (r-1)/2 conjugate pairs. The corresponding complex
simple summands of F(U) are distinct and multiplicity one. Thus the transported
involution necessarily swaps at least one pair of local simples and is NOT split.
This argument applies to EVERY coherent algebra identification for EVERY
simple-fixing parent involution satisfying the setup; changing coherence cannot
alter the real graded-algebra argument. It does NOT exclude a real datum on D
which is not induced from a simple-fixing parent datum.

4. Uniform proposed application to the type-A test.
Take C=C(sl_3,3m), m>=1, H=C_3 generated by J=3m Lambda_1, and
U=m Lambda_1+m Lambda_2. The affine Dynkin coordinates of U are (m,m,m),
so J fixes it and its stabilizer is all C_3. Its center grading is m+2m=0 mod 3,
so it centralizes H and its induced summands are local. Galindo supplies the
coherent simple-fixing reverse-braided parent bar. Therefore the directly induced
bar swaps two of the three resolved U simples at EVERY level 3m.
The m=1 local category is the pointed three-fermion category (Edie-Michell,
Lemma 2.15), which DOES have a split real form. This is an explicit warning:
the induced-bar failure is a failure of the proposed route, NOT a counterexample
to split real forms of the local category.
